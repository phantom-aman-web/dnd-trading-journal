## 1. Methodology & Reconciliation of Requirement Counts

### 1.1 The Requirement Inventory Rebuild
The previous audit reconciliation tracked **81 items**, but an investigation of the governing roadmap revealed a critical phase boundary conflict:
- In the previous report, items `P4-G02` (Multi-Panel Desktop Workspace), `P4-G03` (Draggable Floating Mini-Widgets), `P4-G04` (Quick Notes Floating Tool), and `P4-G05` (Focus Timer Floating Tool) were listed under Phase 4.
- In the authoritative governing roadmap (`five-phase-upgrade.md`, Part VIII lines 1624–1715), **Multi-Panel Workspace** is explicitly specified under **Phase 5 Section D**, and **Floating Mini Widgets** are explicitly specified under **Phase 5 Section E**.
- To restore roadmap integrity, those four items have been formally reclassified to Phase 5 in the separate [phase1-4-phase-boundary-audit.md](file:///c:/Users/hp/Downloads/DnD_COMPLETE_LATEST_20260915/phase1-4-phase-boundary-audit.md).
- Simultaneously, the governing roadmap's explicit Phase 1–4 requirements from Part II (Critical Problems 2.1–2.13), Part IV (Page-by-Page Audit 4.1–4.9, 4.11–4.12), and Part VIII (Implementation Scope Phases 1–4) have been extracted without grouping or omission.

### 1.2 Status Taxonomy
In strict compliance with the governing rules, every requirement is evaluated against the live repository using only the following statuses:
- **`FULLY VERIFIED`**: Implemented, automated tests passing, AND verified in browser/UI environment.
- **`IMPLEMENTED — NOT BROWSER VERIFIED`**: Fully implemented in code, passing unit/integration tests, but live Chrome/browser verification is unavailable in the execution environment.
- **`PARTIAL`**: Core logic exists, but specific roadmap criteria or edge cases remain incomplete.
- **`MISSING`**: Roadmap requirement has no implementation in the codebase.
- **`BLOCKED`**: Implementation cannot proceed due to external dependency or missing infrastructure.
- **`OUT OF SCOPE`**: Feature belongs to Phase 5 or is permanently excluded.
